# Week 1 & Week 2 Business Insights

## Project
Hotel Booking Customer Retention and Dynamic Pricing Analysis

---

## Week 1 – Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering

### Tasks Performed
- Loaded the hotel booking dataset into Python.
- Checked dataset shape, data types, and missing values.
- Removed duplicate records.
- Handled missing values.
- Removed ADR outliers using the IQR method.
- Created new business features:
  - Total Stay Nights
  - Total Guests
  - Total Revenue

### Business Value
- Improved overall data quality.
- Created meaningful features for better analysis.
- Prepared a clean dataset for Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

---

## Week 2 – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

### Analyses Performed
- Hotel Type Distribution
- Booking Cancellation Analysis
- Monthly Booking Trends
- Lead Time Analysis
- ADR (Average Daily Rate) Analysis
- Market Segment Analysis
- Correlation Analysis

### Key Business Insights
- City Hotels received more bookings than Resort Hotels.
- Customers with higher Lead Time were more likely to cancel their bookings.
- Higher ADR values showed a greater chance of booking cancellation.
- Online Travel Agencies generated the highest number of bookings.
- Customers with more Special Requests were less likely to cancel.
- Parking space requests also showed a negative relationship with cancellations.

---

## Business Recommendations
- Monitor high Lead Time bookings to reduce cancellations.
- Improve customer retention strategies for high-risk bookings.
- Optimize room pricing using ADR trends.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing market segments.
